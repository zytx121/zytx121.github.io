I am Yue Zhou, an Associate Professor in the DREAMS Lab at East China Normal University. My research focuses on UAV-based spatial intelligence, remote-sensing vision-language models, adversarial robustness, and oriented object detection. Previously, I was a Research Fellow at [S-Lab, Nanyang Technological University](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/s-lab), and I received my Ph.D. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University under the supervision of [Professor Xue Jiang](https://icisee.sjtu.edu.cn/jiaoshiml/jiangxue.html).

I led the initial release of [MMRotate](https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmrotate) and have led its development since 2022. I also contribute to [AlphaRotate](https://github.com/yangxue0827/RotationDetection), [JDet](https://github.com/Jittor/JDet), and [MMDetection](https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmdetection).
